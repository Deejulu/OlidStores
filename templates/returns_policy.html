Your Satisfaction is Our Priority

At E-Stores, we want you to love your purchase! If you're not completely satisfied, we offer a hassle-free returns and refunds process.

✅ 14-day return window | 💯 100% money-back guarantee | 🚚 Free return shipping on defective items

Return Eligibility

You can return items within 14 days of delivery if they meet the following conditions:

  • Item is in its original condition, unused, and unworn
  • Original packaging, tags, and labels are intact
  • Proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation) is provided
  • Item is not on the non-returnable list (see below)

⚠️ Non-Returnable Items: Undergarments, cosmetics, perishable goods, personalized items, and opened hygiene products cannot be returned for health and safety reasons.

How to Return an Item

Follow these simple steps to initiate a return:

1Contact Us

Email us at returns@estores.com or use our live chat with your order number and reason for return.

2Get Return Authorization

We'll send you a Return Authorization Number (RAN) and return instructions within 24 hours.

3Pack & Ship

Pack the item securely in its original packaging. Include your RAN inside. Ship to the address provided.

4Inspection & Refund

Once we receive and inspect your return (2-3 business days), we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days.

Refund Methods

Refunds are processed using your original payment method:

  • Online Payments (Paystack): Refund to original card/account within 5-7 business days
  • Bank Transfer: Direct refund to your provided account details within 3-5 business days
  • Pay on Delivery: Bank transfer to your account (provide account details when initiating return)

Shipping fees are non-refundable except in cases of defective or incorrect items.

Exchanges

Want to exchange for a different size or color?

  • Contact us within 14 days of delivery
  • Exchange subject to stock availability
  • We'll arrange pickup and delivery of the replacement item
  • No additional shipping fees for exchanges within the same price range

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item:

  • Report the issue within 48 hours of delivery
  • Provide photos of the damage or defect
  • We'll arrange free pickup and provide a full refund or replacement
  • No return shipping fees for defective items

📸 Tip: Take photos or videos when unboxing to document any issues for faster resolution.

Wrong Item Received

If you received the wrong item:

  • Contact us immediately with your order details
  • We'll arrange immediate pickup of the incorrect item
  • The correct item will be shipped to you at no extra cost
  • Full refund if the correct item is out of stock

Cancellations

You can cancel your order before it ships:

  • Log into your account and go to Order History
  • Click "Cancel Order" on pending orders
  • Refund processed within 3-5 business days
  • Contact support if the cancel button is not available

⚠️ Orders cannot be cancelled once shipped. You'll need to initiate a return instead.

Return Shipping Costs

Return shipping fees vary by situation:

  • Change of Mind: Customer pays return shipping (₦1,000-₦2,000)
  • Wrong Size/Color: Exchange shipping covered by E-Stores
  • Defective/Damaged Items: Free return pickup
  • Wrong Item Sent: Free return pickup and replacement shipping
