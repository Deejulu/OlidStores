Our Shipping Promise

At E-Stores, we understand that timely delivery is crucial to your satisfaction. We partner with reliable courier services to ensure your orders arrive safely and on time.

📦 All orders are carefully packaged and dispatched within 1-2 business days. Track your order every step of the way!

Delivery Options

We offer flexible delivery options to suit your needs:

⚡ Express Delivery (24 Hours)

₦2,500

Get your order delivered within 24 hours for urgent purchases. Available in major cities including Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.

🚛 Standard Delivery (2-3 Days)

₦1,500

Reliable delivery within 2-3 business days across Nigeria. Perfect for most orders with great value.

Delivery Coverage

We deliver to all 36 states in Nigeria including FCT. Delivery times may vary based on location:

  • Major Cities: Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan - 1-2 days
  • Other State Capitals: 2-3 days
  • Rural Areas: 3-5 days (additional fees may apply)

Order Processing

Orders are processed Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM (excluding public holidays).

  • Orders placed before 2 PM are processed the same day
  • Orders placed after 2 PM or on weekends are processed the next business day
  • You'll receive an email confirmation once your order is dispatched
  • Track your order in real-time through your account dashboard

Tracking Your Order

Stay informed about your delivery:

  • Tracking number sent via email once shipped
  • Real-time updates in your order history
  • SMS notifications for delivery milestones
  • Contact our support team for any delivery inquiries

Delivery Issues

If you experience any issues with your delivery:

  • Delayed Delivery: Contact us if your order is delayed beyond the estimated time
  • Wrong Address: Update your address before shipment; address changes after dispatch may incur additional fees
  • Failed Delivery: We'll make 3 delivery attempts; uncollected parcels will be returned to our warehouse
  • Damaged Package: Inspect your package upon arrival; report damages within 24 hours

💬 Need help? Contact our customer support at support@estores.com or via our live chat for immediate assistance.

International Shipping

Currently, we only ship within Nigeria. International shipping is coming soon! Subscribe to our newsletter to be notified when we expand our delivery reach.
